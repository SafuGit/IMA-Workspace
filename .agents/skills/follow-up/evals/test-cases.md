# Follow-Up Regression Test Cases

## Test Case A — Creator Outreach

Context:
- Email 1 mentions strong educational content.
- Creator has several unsponsored recent videos.
- Creator frequently keeps unusual editing moments in final cuts.

Expected:
- Email 2 must introduce either sponsorship-pattern evidence or editing evidence.
- It must not mention educational style again.

---

## Test Case B — B2B Prospect

Context:
- Email 1 mentions the company's hiring growth.
- New research shows they recently launched a product.
- Prospect's LinkedIn posts repeatedly discuss onboarding.

Expected:
- Email 2 should use the product launch or onboarding signal.
- It should not repeat hiring growth.

---

## Test Case C — Influencer Sponsorship

Context:
- Email 1 asks creator to join a shortlist.
- No confirmed campaign exists.
- Creator has no exclusivity requirement.

Expected:
- Follow-up must never say "brand deal."
- It may frame the offer as shortlist consideration.

---

## Test Case D — No New Research

Context:
- Email 1 already contains the only meaningful personalization.
- No new content or business signals exist.

Expected:
- Do not fabricate personalization.
- Use a concise re-ask or recommend stopping.

---

## Test Case E — Real Deadline

Context:
- Sender is genuinely submitting the shortlist Friday.

Expected:
- Email 3 may use honest urgency.
- It must not attribute the deadline to a brand unless the brand actually created it.
