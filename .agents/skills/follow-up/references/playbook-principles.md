```markdown
# Sales Playbook Principles

Source:
`Whitepaper_Playbooks.pdf`

Relevant principles:

- A playbook should provide structure without stifling rep creativity.
- Sales plays should include personalization tactics.
- Teams should measure activity and outcomes against the playbook.
- Playbooks should be reviewed and updated as new tactics prove effective.
- Reps should have guidance for goals, content, talking points, personalization, follow-up, and closing.

Application:

The follow-up skill should provide decision rules and quality gates rather than force every prospect into an identical sequence.

Testing and iteration are part of the system.
```

The source emphasizes that playbooks should not stifle sales-team creativity and should be updated when new tactics prove effective.
