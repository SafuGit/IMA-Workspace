# Follow-Up Principles

## Source: AI Outreach Machine Training Document

The source emphasizes:

- Follow-up is an important part of cold outreach.
- Follow-up emails are valuable real estate and should not be wasted.
- Different angles, offers, guarantees, pain points, benefits, proof, lead magnets, case studies, first lines, jokes, ideas, images, copy, and subject lines can be tested.
- The source describes a five-email sequence with the first follow-up as a same-thread bump and later messages testing different angles.
- The source also explicitly encourages testing rather than assuming one message is universally optimal.

The skill adapts these principles by making "new value/new reason to reply" the stronger operating rule.
