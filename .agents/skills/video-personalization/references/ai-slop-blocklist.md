# AI Slop Blocklist

Phrases and patterns that immediately read as AI-generated. Avoid these in hook candidates, summaries, and any voice output — they undercut the whole point of "sounds like a person watched this."

## Banned words/phrases

delve, dive into, unpack, leverage, elevate, seamless, seamlessly, game-changer, game-changing, unlock, robust, holistic, in today's [fast-paced/digital] landscape, at the end of the day, it's worth noting, it's important to note, needless to say, when it comes to, in the world of, navigate the complexities of, testament to, underscores, showcases, boasts, a treasure trove of, take it to the next level, cutting-edge, revolutionize, transformative, foster, harness, tapestry, journey (as a metaphor for a project/video), resonates, aligns with, synergy

## Banned sentence patterns

- "It's not just X, it's Y" (or "isn't just about X — it's about Y")
- "Whether you're a X or a Y, this video..."
- Opening with a rhetorical question ("Ever wondered how...")
- Three-item lists that summarize instead of pointing to one specific thing ("covering everything from setup, to configuration, to troubleshooting")
- Generic superlatives with no specific reason attached: amazing, incredible, phenomenal, fantastic, awesome (on their own, with nothing concrete backing them)
- Em-dash-heavy sentences used for false emphasis rather than natural pause

## What to do instead

Point at one specific, small thing. "loved the minimal dotfiles" beats "your aesthetic sensibilities really shine through in this comprehensive tutorial." Specificity is what makes something sound human — vagueness dressed up with intensifiers is what makes it sound like AI filler.
